A Day in the Life

Structure that sets boys free.

Rise & shine

Morning PT, then Chota Haziri

The day opens on the field with physical training, followed by the old Brownian ritual of Chota Haziri — warm milk, brown bread and honey — before breakfast.

For Parents

Peace of mind, by design.

Sending your son away is the biggest decision a family makes. Every part of boarding life at Col. Brown is built so that he is known, looked after and never alone.

A House Master who lives with him

Every house has a full-time resident House Master or House Mother, with wardens on duty round the clock.

A 20-bed Wellness Centre

A resident nurse, a qualified doctor visiting every day, and 24/7 medical support with full health records.

Six nourishing meals a day

A nutritionist-planned menu, from Chota Haziri after morning PT to a wholesome dinner in the dining hall.

Pastoral care, every hour

A dedicated pastoral team and counselling centre, with 50+ support programmes for his emotional wellbeing.

Admissions 2027–28

His journey begins with three steps.

Admission is offered from Class IV to XII, purely on merit.

01

Register online

Complete the registration form. You'll then receive the prospectus, a sample paper and the syllabus.

02

Entrance test & interview

An online test in English, Hindi and Mathematics (Class IX adds Science), followed by a conversation with your son.

03

Welcome to Col. Brown

On selection, your son joins his house — and a brotherhood that lasts a lifetime.
